Zeus SDR community plugin

PowerStation

Your Shelly relays, dimmers, colour lights and energy meters, controlled and automated right inside Zeus.

  • ⏻One panel for plugs, relays, Pro 4PM, dimmers, RGBW strips and ShellyEM meters, Gen1 to Gen4
  • ▦Scenes: set a whole station, levels and colours in one click, with All off
  • ⚙Rules on Zeus start and close, band, frequency, idle time and time of day
  • ●On-air light follows TX, and everything else waits until you're back on RX
  • ⏱Safety timers: outputs switch themselves off if Zeus stops
  • ⚡Live watts, volts and amps with normal ranges and an event log
